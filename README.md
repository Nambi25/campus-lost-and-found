## Problem Statement

### The Problem

Students frequently lose items such as ID cards, earphones, calculators, bags, wallets, and other personal belongings on campus. Currently, lost-and-found information is often shared through WhatsApp groups or informal communication, making it difficult to search through old messages, identify matching items, and verify ownership.

### Why We Chose This Problem

Lost items are a common problem in college campuses, and existing communication channels are not designed specifically for searching and matching lost belongings. We wanted to build a centralized platform where students can easily report lost or found items and discover potential matches.

## Solution

Campus Lost & Found provides a centralized platform where students can upload details and images of lost or found items. Users can search and filter items by category, location, description, and status.

The system can also use locally running AI/computer-vision models to compare uploaded item images and identify potentially matching lost and found items.

### Key Features

* Report lost items with images and descriptions
* Report found items with images and details
* Search and filter lost/found items
* Location and timestamp information
* AI-powered image similarity matching
* Claim and ownership verification
* Item status tracking
* Centralized campus Lost & Found platform
